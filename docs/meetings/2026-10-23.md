## 2. Agenda and discussion
1. **Student B experiment (Market Risk Index)** – presented results on the real Kraken snapshot:
   - Problem: a level model could not beat the persistence baseline ("risk next week = risk today").
   - Fix: a residual-on-persistence design, with the forecast equal to MRI(t) plus a shrunk predicted change.
   - Tuning: Optuna with purged time-series cross-validation.
   - Result: test MAE 3.70 vs 3.91 for persistence (5.3 % better), with the same margin in cross-validation and validation.
   - Weakness: worse than persistence on BTC and ETH.
2. **Token scope.** Members raised the preference to work on **Bitcoin**. Discussed whether all models must cover all eight tokens.
3. **Missing-feature behaviour.** Measured degradation for the Market Risk model: if Student C's features are unavailable the error rises to 9.26. Agreed that Student C's `/features` endpoint is critical, and that services should cache peer responses.
